Lead story — Shenzhen · APEC

THREE POWERS,
ONE TABLE

Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen today — the first three-way summit of the world's three nuclear powers, on the sidelines of APEC. On the table: nuclear arms control and the Iran war.

New START, the last US–Russia nuclear treaty, expired in February. Washington calls the talks "the beginning of a dialogue"; officials on all sides say no deal is expected today.

Shenzhen, host of APEC 2026. The flags of the United States, China and Russia over one table — the first trilateral of the three sitting presidents.
